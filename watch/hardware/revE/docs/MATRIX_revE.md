# PitchIQ watch rev E: requirements-to-evidence matrix

Date: 2026-11-23. Nothing has been built. PASS means "the design files meet the criterion and the evidence was produced in this revision", never "it works". BLOCKED means the question cannot be answered without a real part, a print, a supplier's reply or a bench test. Paths are relative to `watch/hardware/revE/` unless they start with `firmware/`.

Severity: C critical (board or watch does not function), M major (function degraded or rework), m minor.
