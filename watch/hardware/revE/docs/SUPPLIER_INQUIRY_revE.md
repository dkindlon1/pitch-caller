Subject: Pre-order engineering questions: 6-layer 0.8 mm board, 30.6 x 36.0 mm, 5 pcs, with assembly

Hello,

I am preparing an order of 5 prototype boards and would like your engineering assessment before ordering. Files are attached. Please do not start production; this is a request for evaluation and a quotation only.

Board: 30.6 x 36.0 mm, 6 layers, 0.8 mm finished thickness, FR-4 TG150 or higher, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner copper, ENIG, minimum via 0.2 mm drill / 0.5 mm pad, all vias tented, minimum track and space 0.127 mm. Four vias sit inside the exposed pad of a QFN-32 and need to be filled and capped. One edge has a 10.3 x 6.3 mm notch with 0.5 mm radius relief circles at its two inside corners. Four plated slots (0.50 x 1.10 mm). Silkscreen on the bottom side must be printed (battery polarity).

Assembly: 46 machine-placed parts, 23 part numbers: 38 on the top side, 8 on the bottom side (three SOD-323 diodes, four 0402 resistors, one side-actuated tactile switch). Smallest parts: 0402, QFN-32 at 0.5 mm pitch, LGA-12 2 x 2 mm at 0.5 mm pitch, SOT-23, 3215 crystal. A radio module, a USB-C connector and a flex connector are fitted by us afterwards and are not in the BOM.

Questions:

1. Can you build 6 layers at 0.8 mm finished thickness with this copper and ENIG? Which exact stack-up would you use, and is it a standard one?
2. Are filled-and-capped vias (via-in-pad) available at 0.2 mm drill / 0.5 mm pad at this thickness? Included in the price or extra (amount)? Extra days?
3. Can you hold +/-0.1 mm on the outline at the edge notch? What is your smallest inside-corner radius or router bit? Is the 0.5 mm relief radius acceptable as drawn?
4. Can you make the four plated slots as drawn? Minimum width and tolerance?
5. Panel: will you panelise this board for assembly, at what fee, with tab routing and mouse bites (not V-cut)? What rail width, fiducials and tooling holes do you add? May we send our own panel instead?
6. What is the smallest single-board size you accept for (i) single-sided and (ii) double-sided machine assembly, and what extra fees apply to double-sided?
7. If only the top side is machine-assembled and we hand-fit the 8 bottom-side parts, is there any issue with leaving the bottom unpopulated?
8. Can you place the bottom-side parts listed above, and the 3215 crystal and LGA-12 on the top side?
9. Please list every fee for 5 boards in three scenarios: (a) bare boards; (b) top side assembled; (c) both sides assembled. Include setup, stencil, part loading, panel, engineering, test and parts.
10. One IC (Nordic nPM1300-QEAA-R7) may have to be supplied by us from the USA. What is the process, the address, the handling or customs fee, and the spare quantity you need?
11. If a part is out of stock, do you stop and ask before substituting? How long will you wait?
12. How do you verify rotation and polarity (diodes, QFN and LGA pin 1, the switch)? Will I receive a placement drawing or preview to approve before production, and photos of the first assembled board before shipping?
13. Build time and delivery time to New York, USA for each scenario; shipping options and prices for a parcel under 1 kg.
14. How are US import duties handled for bare and assembled boards (prepaid or on delivery, rate used)?
15. Any design-for-manufacture or design-for-assembly problem you see in the attached files.

Thank you.
