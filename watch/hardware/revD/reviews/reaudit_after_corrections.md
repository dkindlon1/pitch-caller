Method. MEASURED = my own CadQuery/OCC scans and boolean/distance tests on cad/step and cad/step_mjf (scripts in /tmp/reaudit: lib.py, pairs.py, walls.py), pcbnew on kicad/pitchiq_watch.kicad_pcb (origin 100,80), or a section of the downloaded KiCad 3D model. ## 0. Result in one paragraph

Six of the eight mechanical corrections are real as stated. Two are real but fall short of what was claimed: the button gland leaves a 0.16 mm lip on the outer side (claim was at least 0.2 mm each side), and the button plunger does not retract (head travel 0.05 mm), so the board passes the plunger only by squeezing a pad that starts 0.01 mm from the switch stem. The J1 entry-direction dispute is settled in the designer's favour by measurement. Three new defects were found, all one-line parameter fixes (section 3). The charger recovery code is present, matches the stock driver's register map, and the build logs post-date the last source change. No board-order gate was closed by these corrections: the panel, fiducials, J1 real-panel check, SW1 actuator and JLCPCB answers are still open.

## 3. New defects introduced or exposed (each with its exact fix)

- N1 (MAJOR, mechanical, both sets): button gland outer lip 0.16 mm, inner land 0.25 mm. The wall is 1.8 mm, the counterbore takes 0.5, the gland 0.89, leaving 0.41 to split. Fix in build.py and params.py: thicken the wall locally at the button: add a boss of about 5 mm diameter by 0.8 mm on the outer face (wall at the button 2.6 mm), lengthen the plunger by 0.8 so the cap still stands 0.6 proud, and place the groove so each side keeps at least 0.6 (0.6 + 0.89 + 0.5 counterbore + 0.6 = 2.6). Alternative: move the gland into the plunger boss and drop the counterbore.
- N2 (MINOR, fit-check usefulness): step_mjf keeps the port window at 0.15 mm although MJF is +-0.25, so the printed plate may not seat. Fix: add `plate_gap=0.40` to the MJF dict in params.py (only if the plate is printed; the plate is CNC or SLA in MECH_revD.md, then print the case only and keep the plate separate).
- N3 (MINOR, MJF only): web between the screw pilot and the bar hole is 0.50 in the MJF set (0.64 nominal). Fix: `bar_from_face=3.5` (web 0.94 nominal, 0.80 MJF; lug length 5.6 leaves 1.45 beyond the hole edge), then re-check strap clearance.
- N4 (REPORTING, not geometry): report.md "flex bend start" shows 1.03 (real 0.89) and "pocket clear of the gasket" shows 0.22 (real y 0.18). Fix the formula at build.py:191: use R=(sep-t)/2 instead of the fixed 0.45, and report the minimum of the y and z clearances.
- N5 (LABEL): params plunger_oring=(1.5, 0.5) while the model ring has inner diameter 2.0 and OD 2.7 drawn compressed (MEASURED button_oring.step: material from r=1.0 to 1.35, x 31.85..32.35). The part to buy is not defined by the file (audit C UA7 still stands).

## 4. J1, display connector (audit A F1): independent verification

MEASURED, downloaded Hirose_FH12-10S-0.5SH 3D model (STEP, 3 solids, bbox x +-4.55, y -4.4..2.0, z 0..2.0 in model coordinates), sectioned by point scans:
- The actuator solid is at model y -4.4..-1.4 (z 0.15..2.0). The thin cable slot (void at z about 0.4 between housing layers) runs from model y -1.1 to -3.4, that is the model's -y side. The terminal/tail solid (x +-4.55, y -2.0..2.0, z 0..1.8) lies on the model +y side, which has no slot.
- Footprint file (FROM FILE /usr/share/kicad/footprints/Connector_FFC-FPC.pretty/...): signal pads at y=-1.85, mounting pads at y=+1.4, F.Fab body outline y -1.2..4.4 with a stepped actuator outline at y 3.4..4.4. The pads on the footprint's -y side match the tails on the model's +y side, and the Fab actuator at footprint +y matches the model's actuator at -y. So footprint y = -model y (consistent in two independent features) and the slot and actuator are on the footprint's +y side. This is the designer's reading.
- Board (MEASURED pcbnew): J1 at (14.0, 31.0), rotation 0, layer F.Cu. Footprint +y is board +y, which is the lower board edge (y=36). So the cable slot faces the lower edge (south). Pad 1 is at x=11.75 (west), pad 10 at x=16.25 (east, LCD_SCK); MP pads at x=9.85 and 18.15.
- Fold logic (FROM FILE design.py:92-97 and Sharp facts as p1 read them; I did not re-read Sharp): tail leaves the lower glass edge heading south with terminal 1 on the east (right from the front) and contacts on the back face; a 180 degree fold about an east-west axis keeps east and west and turns the contacts to face up. The tail then enters the connector from the south, contacts up: top-contact type, terminal 1 at the east end. Footprint rotation 0 puts pad 10 east, so pad n = terminal 11-n. design.py:98-101 maps T1 to pad 10 through T10 to pad 1: consistent. Net check FROM FILE check_output/pcbnew: pad 3 is LCD_EXTMODE (= terminal 8) and pad 1 is GND (= terminal 10, VSSA); both agree with the LS013B7DH03 pin order recorded in design.py.
- Auditor's CRITICAL F1 (cable entry faces north) is not supported by the 3D model and the footprint; I rate it refuted for the entry direction. Confidence: about 95 percent for entry direction and pad 1 position as drawn in KiCad's library model; about 75-80 percent for the whole chain, because the chain still rests on Sharp's terminal-1-on-the-right and contacts-on-the-back statements that I did not re-read, and on the FH12A (top contact) land pattern being equal to the FH12 footprint (design.py says it is not checked on Hirose's drawing).
- What a real panel must still confirm: terminal 1 side and contact face on the actual tail; tail thickness at the contacts (the FH12 family expects about 0.3 mm FPC with stiffener; the model assumes 0.12 EST; NOT CHECKED against Hirose); tail length (the modelled fold needs roughly 6 mm from the glass edge, my estimate); whether the flip-lock can be latched with the board seated (audit B B4(3), unresolved); and a power-off continuity check pad 1/2 to panel VSS before first power.

## 8. Verdicts
