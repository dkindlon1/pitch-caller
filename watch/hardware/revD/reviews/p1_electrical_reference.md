## (c) CONFIRMED

All PS v1.3 unless stated.

nPM1300 (U2, design.py lines 49-59):
- **Every pin number and name matches Table 34 QFN32:** 1 VOUT1, 2 PVSS1, 3 SW1, 4 PVDD, 5 SW2, 6 PVSS2, 7-11 GPIO0-4, 12 VDDIO, 13 SDA, 14 SCL, 15 SHPHLD, 16 VSET2, 17 VSET1, 18 NTC, 19 VBAT, 20 VSYS, 21 VBUS, 22 VBUSOUT, 23 CC1, 24 CC2, 25-27 LED0-2, 28 LSIN1, 29 LSOUT1, 30 LSIN2, 31 LSOUT2, 32 VOUT2, exposed pad AVSS. CONFIRMED (PS 9.1.1 Table 34; also the three third-party netlists).
- **VSET2 150 k = 3.0 V:** PS s.6.3.2 Table 18 (150 kOhm -> 3.0 V; max 5% tolerance, Config 1 uses 150 k +-1%); R2 150 k CONFIRMED.
- **VSET1 grounded = BUCK1 off:** Table 17 "<100 Ohm (grounded): 0 V (OFF)"; PS s.6.3.1 "If the pin is grounded, BUCK is disabled"; and "Do not leave VSET[n] floating". R1 0R CONFIRMED. If a buck is disabled at power-up, the system defaults to software control of the bucks, so firmware must never enable BUCK1 (s.6.3.1).
- **Buck inductor requirement:** Table 19: 2.2 uH, tolerance <= 20%, DCR <= 400 mOhm, Isat > 350 mA, Imax > 200 mA. L2 value, tolerance suffix "M", DCR 150 mOhm typ CONFIRMED; Isat is NOT FOUND (see below).
- **Buck output capacitor:** Table 20: effective >= 4 uF, ESR <= 50 mOhm. C10 10 uF (10 V X5R) plus C1/C2/C11 on 3V0 meets this at 3 V bias (my estimate; no Samsung DC-bias curve read).
- **BUCK1 and BUCK2 outputs must not be tied together** (s.6.3): not tied. CONFIRMED.
- **NTC:** 10 kOhm with B25/50 = 3380 K is a supported type (Table 11); ADCNTCRSEL reset value 0x01 = 10 kOhm; max capacitor in parallel 100 pF (none fitted). PS s.3.4: pack must carry over/under-voltage and over-current protection and a thermal fuse "if NTC thermistor is not present"; the protected pack plus NTC meets it. If NTC were absent: "tied directly to ground or through a resistor" and disable with BCHGDISABLESET. Murata NCP15XH103F03RC = 10 k / 3380 K is FROM MEMORY.
- **SHPHLD:** internal pull-up RSHPHLD 50 kOhm typ; VIL 0.4 V, VIH 1.2 V to VBAT; "A push button to GND is required" to exit ship mode; tRESETBUT 10 s power-cycle press. Button to GND (SW1 pin 1 to BTN) with TVS D3 CONFIRMED; no external pull-up needed.
- **GPIO defaults:** "set as input with weak pull-down", supplied by VDDIO (s.6.6). Unconnected GPIO0, 2, 3, 4 are safe; GPIO1 is software-configured as the interrupt output (our dts `pmic-int-pin = <1>`, same as Zephyr p2d.dts line 93). Abs-max for digital pins is VDDIO + 0.3 V: the DevZone 128419 failure was VSYS leaking into GPIO1 through its ESD diode; nothing in rev D feeds a PMIC GPIO from VSYS. CONFIRMED.
- **VDDIO:** operating 1.7 V to VSYS (Table 6); 3V0 qualifies. TWI pull-ups: Nordic BOM lists R1/R2 as optional TWI pull-ups "dependent on bus speed and parasitic capacitances"; 4.7 k to 3V0 gives roughly 0.2 us rise time at 50 pF (my arithmetic). CONFIRMED.
- **VBUS limits:** abs-max VBUS 22 V, VBUSOVP 5.5 V, operating VBUSOP 4.0-5.5 V, VBUSPOR 3.9 V, VBUSBOR 3.8 V (VBAT absent). CONFIRMED.
- **Operating voltage of VBAT/VSYS/PVDD/VDDIO: abs-max 5.5 V** (Table 4); VSYS follows VBUS, so keep VBUS <= 5.5 V. CONFIRMED.
- **VSYS from VBUS with an empty cell:** s.6.1.1: "It supplies VSYS but does not regulate its voltage. VBUS voltage is seen at VSYS as a supply, if the VBUS voltage is within specified limits and higher than VBAT by at least VBUSVALID" (210 mV rising). Overview: dynamic power-path "to ensure immediate system operation from VBUS if the battery is depleted". So the MCU can boot from VBUS alone at 100 mA, subject to item 2.
- **CC lines straight from the connector; Rd 5.1 k internal; thresholds VRDCONN 0.2 V, VRD1A5 0.66 V, VRD3A 1.23 V; debounce 15 ms.** CONFIRMED (s.6.1.3, Table 9). Charger-type detection is by software read of USBCDETECTSTATUS; the limit never rises automatically.
- **Exposed pad = AVSS (ground)** CONFIRMED (Table 34).
- **Charging defaults:** ICHG default 32 mA (32-800 mA, 2 mA steps); ITERM 10% default; VTRICKLE_FAST default 2.9 V; VBATPOR 2.75 V, VBATBOR 2.4 V; BCHGVTERMNORM reset code 0x02 (voltage for that code not found in the text). 75 mA from our dts is inside the range and below the 100 mA VBUS startup limit.
- **Charging must be enabled by the host** (BCHGENABLESET) and starts in trickle: CONFIRMED (s.6.2.1). With VBUS present and no battery, "the host software will see repeated charger events".

Raytac MDBT50Q-1MV2 (U1, lines 24-42):

## (d) NOT FOUND (could not be verified from a document I read)

1. **Rd presence with an unpowered chip / absent or dead cell.** The PS says only "These pins have internal pull-downs with resistance equal to Rd" and "The device is supplied by VBUS or VBAT". Nothing on whether Rd is connected before VBUS appears (a Type-C source applies VBUS only after seeing Rd). Bench test required. (design.py lines 121-125.)
2. **Nordic's own reference schematic figures 59-62** (images in the PDF; only labels and BOM extracted): exact treatment of LSIN/LSOUT when unused, whether SW/VOUT of a disabled buck is left open, VDDIO source, PVSS/AVSS net-tie layout.
3. **A Nordic statement on cold-start from VBUS only with no battery.** Only DevZone 128233 (unanswered) and 125337 (Nordic: 100 mA default, volatile registers, 2.2 uF on VBAT). **The earlier claim of "overshoot" is not supported:** DevZone 128233 reports VSYS dropping to about 2.5 V (undershoot/collapse); 128419 is BUCK2 reading 4.1-4.45 V because VSYS back-fed GPIO1 through its ESD diode to VDDIO (wiring fault, root cause found by the poster, agreed by Nordic) and is not a cold-start overshoot.
4. **Isat of TDK MLP2012H2R2MT0S1** versus the Nordic requirement Isat > 350 mA (Imax > 200 mA): TDK datasheet 403. design.py line 62 itself says "saturation current not published". Read the TDK datasheet (or switch to the Murata DFE201610E-2R2M named there) before ordering.
5. **Maximum allowed buck output or VBUS capacitance** in the PS text (only minima are given): not found.
6. **Voltage that BCHGVTERMNORM reset code 0x02 selects** (default VTERM before firmware runs): the register field table was not read; firmware sets 4.2 V explicitly (dts line 144).
7. **Sharp FPC contact face relative to the viewing side** (figure images) and the Hirose FH12-10S-0.5SH(55) contact orientation (item 4).
8. **SMF5.0A leakage and standoff (Littelfuse), PESD5V0S1BA, 1N4148WS, FC-135 load capacitance and stray assumption (3.5 pF), GCT USB4715 drawing, Samsung DC-bias:** datasheets not read. The 18 pF load capacitors follow 2 x (12.5 pF - 3.5 pF stray) = 18 pF, which is the usual formula (FROM MEMORY).
9. **Raytac reference-circuit capacitor values for the 32.768 kHz crystal and VBUS handling when USB is unused** (figures are images; the claim at line 32 "Raytac reference ties VBUS to GND" is unverified by me).
10. **nRF52840 UICR/firmware requirements** (reset pin enable, DCDC settings): firmware must leave REG0 high-voltage DC/DC off because DCCH is open (FROM MEMORY: CONFIG_BOARD_ENABLE_DCDC_HV must stay off; REG1 DC/DC is fine, Raytac says its inductor is inside).
