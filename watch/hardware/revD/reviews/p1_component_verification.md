## Findings

### CRITICAL
C1. J1 (LCD FPC connector) contact side and pin-1 end do not match the Sharp panel as the stack is drawn.
- Sharp p.23: Fig 8-1 (flat tail) lists bottom-contact connectors (SMK CFP-4610-0150F, Molex 51441-1093); the FPC bend Fig 8-2 (bent back 180 degrees, "do not bend toward the polarizer") lists a TOP-side contact connector (SMK CFP-4510-0150F). The design.py note picks bottom contact from the flat-tail line. The panel sits above the board and J1 (14.0, 31.0) is under it, so the tail must fold back 180 degrees: contact fingers then face up, which needs a top-contact connector. FH12-10S-0.5SH(55) is bottom contact (RS Components listing and JLC page).
- Pin order: Sharp Fig 8-1 (display up, tail at 6 o'clock) shows pin 10 at left and pin 1 at right (east). A fold about the east-west axis keeps pin 1 east. J1 pad 1 is at x = -2.25, west. Pins 1-10 would reverse (SCLK on VSSA, GND on SCLK).
- Fix: confirm the fold on a paper/3D mock-up before ordering. If folded: switch to a top-contact 0.5 mm 10-pos FPC connector (Hirose FH12A-10S-0.5SH(55) is the top-contact sibling, FROM MEMORY, check the drawing and JLC stock) and mirror the pad order or rotate J1 180 degrees so pad 1 is on the east side. If you want to keep FH12-10S (bottom contact), the tail must stay flat and enter from the board edge side: the panel then has to move so its tail reaches J1 without folding. Do not order boards until this is decided.

C2. SW1 side-button actuator faces into the board (needs Alps drawing to confirm).
- KiCad footprint (stock Alps SKRTLAE010): switch body y -1.35..+1.21, button lug to y +2.04 (local +y). Board: bottom side, rot -90 gives pad "2" (local 0,+0.9) at board (-0.9, 0) relative to SW1 (28.5, 12.0). So the actuator points to -x, toward the cell and display interior, not out of the edge at x = 30.6 (switch body ends at x about 29.85).
- Fix: rotate SW1 by 180 degrees (use rot 90 on the bottom side; the pad geometry mirrors) and move it so the lug is flush with the case/edge, then check against the Alps drawing (could not be read) for the actuator side. Confirm in a 3D view before release. Also adjust CPL accordingly.

### MAJOR
M1. CPL rotation convention is wrong for bottom-side parts. out/fab/cpl_jlcpcb.csv holds KiCad's raw orientation. The kicad-jlcpcb-tools plugin (which produces files JLC accepts) writes bottom rotations as (180 - rot) mod 360 and then adds a per-footprint correction (fabrication.py lines 686-697). For J3 (0 raw) that gives 180, SW1 (-90 raw) gives 270, D4-D6 (90) give 90, non-polar R17-R20 (0) give 180 (harmless). J3 and SW1 are polarised/handed: wrong rotation puts J3 opening toward the board interior. Fix: generate the CPL with the plugin (or apply (180-rot) mod 360 to Layer=Bottom rows) and check the J3/SW1 pose in JLC's placement preview.
M2. Top-side polarised/IC parts need JLC per-footprint rotation corrections that are not applied (CPL is raw KiCad). JLC's library zero differs from KiCad for QFN, LGA, SOT-23 and module footprints; the per-footprint offsets are downloaded by the plugin at runtime and I could not read them. Applies to U1, U2, U4, Q1, Q2, D1, D2, D3, J1. Fix: compare each in JLC's placement preview (pin 1 dot vs KiCad pin 1: U2 pin 1 top-left, U4 pin 1 top-left of the 4-pad left column, U1 pad 1 top-left with antenna at top, SOT-23 pin 1 top-left with pin 3 right, SOD cathode pad 1 left) and put the corrected rotations in the CPL. Pin 1 could be 90/180/270 degrees off by default.
M3. Bottom-side J3 (4.21 mm above PCB) exceeds the 4.2 mm limit by 0.01 mm and SW1 3.3 mm, cell about 4.0 mm: tallest bottom parts are very close. Fix: check the enclosure bay with 4.3 mm clearance for J3 (the 4.21 figure is derived from drawing sheet 1 dimensions 4.76 and 0.55 plus Mouser's 4.21, mapping unverified).

### MINOR
m1. J3 contact pads start 0.20 mm behind the notch edge, drawing starts them at the edge (pad 1.05 long). Toe overlap is shorter by 0.2 mm. Fix: ask JLC if 0.1 mm to the edge is accepted for the notch (route-out edge, not a V-cut), otherwise check solder joints on first articles.
m2. U1 module height 2.05 mm and M1 motor 2.1 mm exceed the 2.0 mm top-side limit by 0.05/0.1 mm. Check the LCD standoff (2.7 mm above the board) still clears U1 and M1.
m3. D2 SMF5.0A: Vrwm 5 V against VBUS up to 5.5 V normal: leakage rises above standoff (Vbr min 6.4 V so no clamping). Acceptable; a 5.5 V standoff part (SMF5.0A is already the lowest 5 V part) cannot be raised without the clamp rising to 9.2+ V. Keep as is, note for the charge-current test.
m4. D3-D6 PESD5V0S1BA clamp 18 V (LCSC) is above the 5.5 V pin limit on SHPHLD and any 5.5 V pin; it protects only against large energy, the PMIC's own ESD structure still takes the first pulse. Fix: none needed; do not rely on D3 as full-voltage protection.
m5. M1 ratings 2.7-4.0 V against VSYS that can exceed 4.0 V on USB power (design.py already says firmware caps duty). Fix: enforce duty in firmware such that Vrms <= 4.0 V or add a series resistor.
m6. 10 uF 0603 10 V X5R capacitors at 3.0-5.0 V keep only about 40-60% of 10 uF (FROM MEMORY; Samsung curves could not be fetched). If Nordic's nPM1300 reference asks for 10 uF effective at VSYS/PVDD, use 0805 or 16 V parts. Nordic reference values were not rechecked.
m7. RF: Raytac keep-out is the module's top 3.8 mm and 12.4 mm wide (drawing p.9). The board keep-out runs y -1..4.0 against the module span y 0.65..4.45: 0.45 mm short. Fix: set KEEPOUT[3] to 4.4 in gen_pcb.py (pad row starts at 4.45).
