# Manufacturing (DFM/DFA) review, PitchIQ watch rev B

Scope: out/fab (gerber, drill, bom, cpl), scripts/gen_pcb.py + router.py rules, kicad/pitchiq_watch.kicad_pcb measured read-only with pcbnew, vendor pages fetched 2026-11-23.
Not verified: no JLCPCB/PCBWay quote or DFM upload was run (calculators are interactive and were not reachable), so prices below are only what the pages state. Items marked UNVERIFIED rest on my memory of vendor practice, not on a page I read.

## Findings

### M1 [MAJOR] 24 vias break into pad copper and 54 vias have the hole inside a pad's mask opening
Defect: the router drops signal and plane vias against pad edges. Via centre to pad edge is 0.12 to 0.125 mm for 24 vias (hole radius is 0.125, so the hole breaks the pad edge) and under 0.175 mm for 54 (hole inside the pad mask opening, because the opening is pad + 0.05). With no mask between hole and pad the via is not tented and solder wicks into the barrel.
Evidence: measured with pcbnew (all same-net). 24 breaking: C1.2, C5.1, C5.2, C6.1, C6.2, C7.1, C7.2, C9.1, C9.2, C10.1, C16.2, C18.2, C19.2, C20.2, C24.2, L2.2, L3.2, Q2.2, R5.1, R11.1, R12.2, R13.1, R16.1, U3.12. Examples: R11.1 via at (2.3,26.3) 0.12 from pad; U3.12 via at (16.7,21.2). The mask gerbers carry no via apertures (tented by default) but a tented via cannot stay tented where a pad opening overlaps its hole. JLCPCB assembly FAQ part 2: "Vias on pads can cause weak joints: the solder flows into the vias and may cause weak soldering after reflowing." Most are 0402/0603 passives (tombstone and starved joints), and U3.12 is an OLGA GND pad whose vent holes must stay clear (ADI OLGA design note).
Fix: in router.py add a via-to-pad rule so the hole edge is at least 0.1 mm outside the mask opening: via centre at least 0.125 + 0.05 + 0.10 = 0.275 mm from any pad edge (or move vias onto the plane side of a 0.2 mm stub). Re-run drc. Do not rely on via-in-pad filling: JLCPCB lists it as a separate filled-and-capped process, and its price is not on any page I read.

### M2 [MAJOR] Mask webs are 0.05 to 0.06 mm on U4 and U5 against 0.10 mm minimum
Defect: with 0.05 expansion the mask web between adjacent pads is: LIS2DH12 (U4) 0.05 mm (gap 0.15 minus 0.10), TPS61023 (U5) about 0.06 mm, nPM1300 corner pad pairs under 0.1 mm diagonally, FH12 J1 exactly 0.10 mm (0.2 gap minus 0.10). JLCPCB bridge minimum is 0.10 mm (green). The fab will either delete the web (one gang opening, bridge risk on an LGA) or shrink the openings without telling you.
Evidence: pcbnew pad bounding boxes plus 0.05; gen_pcb.py `ds.m_SolderMaskExpansion = mm(0.05)`; JLCPCB page "Solder mask bridge (1 oz) 0.10 mm", "expansion 1:1".
Fix: set board mask expansion to 0.025 (webs: U4 0.10, U5 0.11, J1 0.15, U2 0.20) or 0 on U4/U5/J1 pads only, and regenerate. Keep green.

### M3 [MAJOR] Via annular ring 0.125 mm is under both fabs' stated minimum
Defect: via 0.50/0.25 gives ring 0.125 mm. JLCPCB: "recommended 0.20, absolute min 0.15 mm". PCBWay: standard minimum annular ring 0.15 mm, outer weld ring 5 mil (0.127) normal, so 0.125 (4.9 mil) falls into "medium". JLCPCB's own same page also lists a "0.15/0.25" minimum via, which contradicts its ring rule; the order form or DFM check will decide, but the first order should not depend on that. gen_pcb.py also sets `m_ViasMinAnnularWidth = 0.1`.
Fix: keep pad 0.50 and change the drill to 0.20 mm (ring 0.15, JLCPCB preferred minimum hole 0.2, no router geometry change; raises hole-to-copper margins). Edit gen_pcb.py `SetViaDrill(mm(0.20))` and router.py `VIA_DRILL = 0.20`, `ds.m_ViasMinAnnularWidth = 0.15`. 0.8 mm board and 0.2 hole is aspect ratio 4. A 0.25 hole with pad 0.55 would instead need re-routing.

### M4 [MAJOR] Assembly class: this board cannot use economic assembly, and the files have no fiducials or rail plan
Defect: JLCPCB Economic assembly is "single-sided placement only"; the board has 5 bottom parts (U3, C14 to C17), so it needs Standard. Standard requires rails ("Necessary"), fiducials ("Necessary") and a single-PCB minimum of 70x70 mm, so a 32x36 board must be ordered as a panel. The design has no fiducials (none in PLACE). Fine-pitch and LGA parts (nPM1300, LIS2DH12, SOT-563) need them.
Evidence: jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities (economic/standard table); specifications-for-adding-process-edges article: 5 mm process edges, 2 mm tooling holes, 1 mm fiducials 3.85 mm from the panel edge. PCBWay: min 10x10, break-away rails on two long edges for non-rectangular boards, "double-sided placement" available.
Fix: order as a JLCPCB-built panel (for example 2x2 with 5 mm rails, 2 mm tooling holes, panel fiducials) with mouse-bite tabs, not V-cut (R3 corners, V-cut needs straight edges). Keep tabs off the antenna end (x 6 to 26, y 0 to 4) and off the top edge near U1. Add 3 local 1.0 mm fiducials (1.0 copper, 2.0 mask opening, no copper keep-in) on each side near corners, for example top at about (2.5,4.5), (29.5,4.5), (2.5,33), bottom at about (2.5,3.5), (29.5,3.5), (29.5,33) after checking space; fiducials must be at least 0.25 from edge. Edge clearance is not an issue: nearest machine-placed pad to the board edge is D3 at 1.25 mm; SW1 and J2 are 1.1 mm; JLCPCB suggests at least 0.3 mm from the edge and about 0.2 mm routing tolerance.

### M5 [MAJOR] CPL angles are raw KiCad angles; no per-part orientation corrections
Defect: out/fab/cpl_jlcpcb.csv is KiCad position data (mm, negative Y) with every ordinary IC and transistor at 0.0 (U2, U4, U5, Q1, Q2, SW1 90, J1 0, U3 0 on Bottom, L1 and L2 180, C8 to C10 180). JLCPCB maps its library part orientation onto the CPL angle, and its model orientation differs from KiCad's footprint for many families (SOT-23, SOT-563, QFN, LGA, connectors) and for bottom-side parts. UNVERIFIED: the community JLC conversion tools carry a rotation table (for example +180 for SOT-23, +270 for QFN/SOIC) and a mirrored-angle rule for bottom parts for this reason. A wrong angle on U2 (QFN32), U4, U5 or U3 gives a wrong-pin-1 board that is not reworkable.
Evidence: cpl_jlcpcb.csv lines for U2, U3, U4, U5, Q1, Q2, J1.
Fix: upload and inspect the JLCPCB 3D/placement viewer for every polarised part before paying (U2, U3, U4, U5, Q1, Q2, D1 to D3, SW1, J1, Y1, C1/C5 orientation irrelevant) and add the correction column per part; pin-1 silk dots exist only if the footprint carries them (see M13). Check that bottom-side rows (C14 to C17, U3) use the same angle convention.

### M6 [MAJOR] U1 (Raytac module) cannot be bought through JLCPCB, and JLCPCB will not ship it back to you
Defect: LCSC C5118826 is out of stock (research/procurement.md: out of stock, from $18.60). A module is not available as a sourced part. JLCPCB's FAQ says it does "not support the unassembled components to be sent with the completed PCBA order"; consigned components "will not be tested ... before or after PCBA production". PCBWay: "We prefer you supply all the parts"; kit with BOM and packing list. The consignment rules, fees and quantity overage for JLCPCB are not on any page I could read, so they are unverified.
Fix: decide before ordering. Option A (recommended for the first batch): JLCPCB assembles everything except U1 (mark DNP in BOM and CPL, hand-solder the module with hot air plus hot plate, the module has castellations and ground LGA pads; use no-clean paste and do not wash). Option B: JLCPCB consignment, ask support first and confirm the stock is ordered ahead for the 2 to 3 week delay. SparkFun sells the MDBT50Q-1MV2 ($8.95 at 1). nPM1300 stock was 921 at LCSC on the day of research, MAX30101 only 53, LIS2DH12 1,485: do not order more boards than the thinnest part allows plus 20 % loss.

### M7 [MAJOR] Raytac module reflow limit is unknown; second-side reflow of U1 is an unchecked risk
Defect: the module spec (Ver.K, Section 2.4) says the reflow profile is in a separate Support download and gives no peak, time above liquidus, or reflow count; Section 10: "Do not wash the module. No-Clean Paste is used in production." This board reflows the bottom side after (or before) the top side. Whether JLCPCB does the top or bottom first is not stated on any page I read.
Fix: get the Raytac profile and maximum reflow count from service@raytac.com before ordering; if two reflows are not allowed, hand-solder U1 last (this also fixes M6 and removes the second pass). If U1 goes through the line on the first pass it sees two reflows, whichever side is first, unless U1 is on the side reflowed last.

### M8 [MAJOR] MAX30101 handling: the standard double-sided flow meets the ADI rules only partly
Evidence (ADI datasheet "Soldering and Cleaning Recommendations"): "should not be cleaned with a liquid solution, baked, or coated with anything"; "If possible, the MAX30101 should be the last component installed"; install "after the board ultrasonic cleaning is completed"; low-residue no-clean paste; package "not sealed from dust or liquid"; reflow max 260 degC. ADI OLGA design note: MSL3, standard SMT process, 100 um electropolished stencil with openings 25 um smaller per side, type 3 or finer SAC305, nitrogen purge recommended, peak 260 degC, profile checked with thermocouples, cleaning not recommended (water enters through the vent holes), vent holes must not be blocked by solder or flux, X-ray voids under 30 %, bake 125 degC 48 h if the humidity card is pink or floor life exceeded, rework not recommended. No public ADI page I read describes a cover tape or Kapton over the window or a multi-reflow limit.
Assessment: (a) reflow itself is allowed (standard process, MSL3, 168 h floor life). (b) "Last component" is met only if JLCPCB reflows the bottom side second: not controllable from the files. (c) Datasheet says no bake, the OLGA note and J-STD-033 say bake at 125 degC after floor life: contradictory, ask ADI; JLCPCB bakes moisture-sensitive LEDs on request only (terms Section 5 item 10), nothing about MSL ICs. (d) MSL3 floor life of cut tape from LCSC is not under your control. (e) Flux residue near the window: ask JLCPCB for no-wash (no cleaning) and nitrogen is not promised (JLCPCB Economic fixed 255 +-5 degC).
Fix: first order, cheapest and safest: have JLCPCB assemble top side plus the four bottom capacitors, mark U3 DNP, and fit U3 yourself last (hot plate, hand paste with a 0.1 mm stencil, no wash, window protected with polyimide tape during any later contact). Second choice: let JLCPCB place U3 and add the order note "bottom side last, no cleaning, bake MAX30101 as per J-STD-033". Check X-ray report for U3 and U2 and U4.

### M9 [MAJOR] Programming and test access is not practical as designed
Defect: the cradle has VBUS, GND, SWDIO, SWDCLK, nRESET (TP1 to TP5, 2 mm, 4 mm pitch, y=28.5) but no target-voltage sense; 3V0 is on TP6, a 1.5 mm pad 15 mm away (5.0,12.0), with TP7 to TP9 (1.5 mm, 3.5 mm pitch). SWD tools need VTref. At first power-up the battery is not connected (JP1 open), so the board is powered only from VBUS through the nPM1300, which is also unverified (power power-path without a battery is not confirmed in research). A pogo jig needs 9 contacts across a 19x16 mm area on a 0.8 mm board that bows under spring force, and the 4 mm pitch is not a catalogue pogo-connector pitch (custom plate).
Fix: put the five cradle pads and 3V0 in one row (add a 6th pad, or move TP6 to the cradle row at x=28), keep 4 mm pitch (2 mm pads give 1 mm placement tolerance) and give the pogo pins a fixture with two locating pins in the rounded outline or two 1.0 mm NPTH holes outside the keep-outs; drive VBUS 5 V from the jig and read VTref from TP6; add a support block under the board behind J1 and the pad row. Check on the first board that the nPM1300 powers up from VBUS alone with JP1 open. TP6 to TP9 stay for bring-up only.

### M10 [MINOR] Hand-soldered wire pads have no strain relief or through-hole anchor
BT1, M1, J2 are SMD pads (1.8x3.0, 1.2x1.8) with no paste (verified: no paste layer on any of BT1, M1, J2, JP1 pad and none on TP1 to TP9). Battery and motor wires pulled during assembly will lift pads. Fix: add a 0.8 to 1.0 mm tie-down NPTH or slot beside each pad or plan tape plus adhesive, and wire length at least 15 mm. JLCPCB wire soldering is a charged manual service and not needed.

### M11 [MINOR] Mask and finish for the bottom cradle are fine but must be ordered as ENIG
Verified in pcbnew: TP1 to TP5 (2.0 mm) and TP6 to TP9 (1.5 mm) have B_Mask and B_Cu only, no paste, exposed copper. JLCPCB 6-layer is ENIG only (no HASL), PCBWay offers ENIG; choose ENIG (not OSP) because pogo contacts and ZIF fingers wear OSP. Also the gold-flash pads lie against the wrist: keep ENIG 2 u" or thicker. No design change.

### M12 [MINOR] Order-form items not carried by the files
gbrjob `"Finish": "None"` and `"BoardThickness": 0.8`: select ENIG, 0.8 mm, 6 layers, green, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner, "no impedance control", "tenting: cover vias" (all vias are 0.25 mm with no mask opening), and an explicit stackup request: JLCPCB's 6-layer impedance page lists stackups only at 1.2/1.6/2.0 mm and no 0.8 mm table, so confirm the 0.8 mm 6-layer build and its dielectric thickness with support (nothing on this board is impedance controlled, but plane spacing sets the capacitance of the antenna ground return). PCBWay 6-layer minimum is 0.9 mm normal, 0.7 mm medium difficulty, so a 0.8 mm board there carries extra cost: use JLCPCB.

### M13 [MINOR] Silkscreen gives no polarity or pin-1 help for the assembler
Designators are on the fab layers (gen_pcb.py), so silkscreen has only footprint outlines and three texts of 0.8 mm height, 0.12 mm line (JLCPCB's silk minimum is typically 0.15 mm line and 1 mm height: UNVERIFIED for this page). JLCPCB: "we cannot recognize the correct polarity" without silkscreen markings. Add pin-1 dots for U1 to U5 and a cathode bar for D1 to D3 on silk, keep the 0.1 mm silk clipped by mask warning list clear, enlarge "+", "-" and "BAT" to 1.0/0.15.

### M14 [MINOR] SW1 boss holes 0.21 mm from copper
Verified: the Alps SKRTLAE010 NPTH bosses (0.9) sit 0.21 mm from SW1 pad 1 and 0.25 mm from pad MP after the script trimmed the pads; JLCPCB NPTH to track minimum is 0.2 mm. Inside the limit, the solder joint area is smaller than the stock land pattern; check the joint on the first board as the script comment says. Hole tolerance +0.13/-0.08 mm for PTH, NPTH tolerance is not stated: 0.21 leaves no margin if the drill wanders. Fix: trim pad 1 to y 0.2 mm further or accept and test.

### M15 [MINOR] nPM1300 exposed pad has no thermal vias
Verified: no via inside the 3.45 mm EP (pad 33, GND); connection depends on the F.Cu GND zone with thermal-relief spokes (0.25 mm, `ZONE_CONNECTION_THERMAL`). The charger and two bucks heat that pad. If vias are added, JLCPCB and PCBWay will not tent them under the pad: use 0.2 mm drill and ask for filled-and-capped, or accept a solid connection via zone `FULL` on the EP only. The 9 x 0.93 mm paste squares (65 %) are acceptable.

### M16 [MINOR] DRC leftovers
out/drc_report.txt: 73 items: 70 library warnings (PQ and empty library), 2 silk clipped by mask, 1 `track_dangling`. The dangling track is real copper; find it and delete it before the Gerbers go out.

### M17 [MINOR] Vias around the optical sensor
Verified: 28 vias within 3 mm of the U3 body; 4 sit inside its outline (14.1,20.3), (15.5,17.5), (15.6,23.4), (16.7,21.2); all are tented by the default mask output (no mask aperture in B_Mask or F_Mask). Plated barrels are opaque; light leakage through tented 0.25 mm holes is a small effect and the mask skin over a hole can be thin (pinholes). Keep the window clear of untented holes and add a black or opaque back cover opening only over the window. The via at U3 pad 12 (M1) is the one that matters for solder. No separate fix beyond M1.

## What to change for a cheaper or more likely first order

1. Order boards at JLCPCB (6 layers, 0.8 mm, ENIG), assemble top side plus bottom capacitors in standard assembly, hand-fit U1 and U3 last (M6 to M8). This avoids consignment, a second-side reflow of the module, and the ADI "last component" conflict. The 4 bottom capacitors C14 to C17 stay on the bottom (they belong next to U3's 1V8 and 5V_LED pins); moving them to the top lengthens the sensor supply loop and would not remove the bottom-side sensor, so it does not buy single-sided assembly unless U3 is also hand-fitted (then JLCPCB economic assembly, single-sided, with its fixed 255 +-5 degC profile, becomes possible: still check that 6-layer 0.8 mm counts as "standard stack-up" there: not stated).
2. Fix M1 to M3 first; they are router and rule changes only.
3. Thickness: 0.8 mm is available at JLCPCB (not at PCBWay without surcharge). A 1.0 mm board does not help the FH12 ZIF (mating depends on FPC thickness, not board thickness) and costs 0.2 mm of stack height; use 0.8 mm but support the board at J1 during FPC insertion and ZIF latch closing.
4. Hand sequence: SMT boards in; inspect X-ray on U2, U4, U3 (JLCPCB applies X-ray automatically for QFN/LGA); program and test on a pogo jig from VBUS before any wire is soldered (M9); solder motor wires at M1; insert the display FPC into J1 and close the latch before the cell covers the area (cell outline x 6.1 to 25.9, y 8.5 to 34.5 overlaps J1 at y 29 to 33); solder battery leads at BT1 (+ left pad), fix the cell with tape and an insulator over the top parts; bridge JP1 with solder last. Fixtures: board-edge nest with pogo plate, a ZIF latch support, a hot plate or hot-air station with Kapton for U3, tweezers and polyimide tape.

## Checked and found correct

- All required layers are present and named for both fabs; copper layer count and order match the 6-layer stack; gbrjob lists six copper files.
- No pad or via lies within 0.25 mm of the board edge (closest hole 1.47 mm); smallest copper-to-edge on any pad 1.1 mm; all minimum trace (0.2) and space (0.15, pads 0.127) values are above JLCPCB 0.09 and PCBWay 5/6 mil outer and 4/5 mil inner limits.
- Hole-to-hole minimum 0.572 mm against JLCPCB 0.2 (via)/0.45 (pad) and PCBWay 11 mil via; via hole to foreign track at least 0.275 mm against 0.2.
- Drill file is consistent: 142 x 0.25 PTH (equals the 142 vias), 2 x 0.9 NPTH, no unplated mixed.
- No paste on BT1, M1, J2, JP1 or TP1 to TP9; every other SMD pad except the nPM1300 EP pad has paste; the EP uses nine 0.93 mm squares.
- TP1 to TP5 are mask-opened on the bottom, ENIG is the only 6-layer finish at JLCPCB, so the pogo pads get the right finish.
- Paste apertures for U4 (0.375x0.35), U5 (0.7x0.34) and U3 (0.75x0.35) are above the usual 0.66 area ratio at 0.12 mm stencils.
- CPL row count matches the claim: 51 top, 5 bottom; hand and DNP parts (BT1, JP1, M1, J2, R12, TP1 to TP9) are excluded from CPL and BOM.
- Component-to-edge distances are all at least 1.1 mm, above the 0.3 mm JLCPCB suggestion.
- LCSC stock of nPM1300 (921) and LIS2DH12 (1,485) is adequate for a prototype run; U3 (53) is thin.
