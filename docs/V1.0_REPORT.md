## What the review found (Phase 9.3)

A fresh-context agent reviewed `v0.9..HEAD` against the plan. **No hitter-boundary violation**: no change touches the
section 2 tables, `P08` entries or functions, `OUTCOME_ADJ`'s `cmd/zsw/osw/wz/wo/cs` are unchanged, and the hitter files
are untouched. The only `outcome_probs` change is the wild-pitch line reading per-level `WP_RATE`, which the plan allows.

Three blockers, all on the calling side. The first two are re-verified in `docs/HANDOFF_hitter_model.md` and **need
Drew's decision**, because each means changing a method the plan specifies:

1. **The usage prior for small-composition arsenals is mis-derived.** `two_pitch` (FB+CB, 5 pitcher-seasons, under the
   40-season minimum) falls back to all-pitcher *kind* shares, so its curveball inherits a four-pitch MLB pitcher's
   curveball rate: at 3-1 the prior is FB 98.2% / CB 1.8% and the engine calls FB **99.24%**, where a flat prior at the
   same costs gives FB 70.9% / CB 29.1%. About **84% of B1's failures are this one arsenal**, and B5's two-pitch failure
   is the same cause. The hitter model cannot fix it: the prior is a multiplicative anchor and the cost term is at most 1.
2. **A `u_T` cell of exactly 0 deletes a pitch type with no why line.** `u_T["FB+SL+CB+CH"]["3-0"]["same"]["CB"] = 0.0`,
   and at 3-0 a grade-5 curveball that is the cheapest call by 0.027 runs and survives both guards gets 0.00% with an
   empty rules dict. That contradicts plan 3.7's promise that a pitch cannot reach 0% without a guard giving a reason.
3. **Python/JS can exceed the 1e-9 tolerance** on situations the parity generator never builds (reported 1 of 300 at
   1.13e-8, traced to `solve_sit_game` stopping at `QRE_TOL = 1e-3` on a near-degenerate 3-2 fixed point). Not
   reproduced with the repo's own generator (400 cases pass, largest 1.43e-10), so unconfirmed but likely.

**Taken together, items 1 and 2 mean the Phase 5 frontier's conclusion is partly wrong.** `results/tune_v10.md` says the
remaining gaps come from the engine's cost differences, i.e. the hitter model, and v1.0 was paused on that basis. A
material share of B1/B2/B5 is ours. The grid should be re-run after Drew rules on items 1 and 2.

Fixed during this session (none change a call distribution): `test-app.sh` ran `golden_v09` second, so with `set -e`
every later check - including the `app/dist` build gate and all the app suites - never ran; CR1 crashed in Python and

## What is not fixed, and why

- **B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B7, B8a, B9 and B13** all fail at the starting values, and no point in the 96-point grid passes.
  Per plan rule 11 nothing was loosened, no term was added and nothing was tuned outside the stated ranges.
- **B13 is a genuine regression** against v0.9 (100% -> 91.7%), the one band the plan added specifically to catch a
  regression. `results/tune_v10.md` traces it to 3-2, where the temperature is about 0.005 x run scale and chase goes to
  0% at both eye 1 and eye 3.
- **B8a** moved the wrong way (73.2% -> 70.3%). Plan section 7 says to report rather than invent a ground-ball bonus.
- **T1-T4** are tracked, not enforced, and belong to the hitting model. T4 is unchanged.
- **The simulator floor** (+0.25 runs/game over 90 paired games) has never been measured for v1.0: the v0.9 baseline
  games are not on this machine, and regenerating them here would not be the baseline the limit was set against.
- **`golden_v10`** is not frozen, because the engine is not final.
- **The CI-tier call sheet and the v1.0 scenarios are not yet wired into `scripts/test-app.sh`** (plan Phase 9.2). Both
  fail today, so wiring them in would break that suite for a second reason; `scripts/v10_gates.sh` runs both instead.
- **The learner switch** was not retuned. Drew kept the current setting and asked for a rerun when Phase 5 resumes.
- **The README engine section** is not rewritten, because the engine's tuned constants will change.
