## 1. What it does

It scores any engine version on real college games, through one interface, with the same data filters and the same
frozen pitcher assumption for every version.

| Metric | Role | Unit scored | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pitch_5way` | **primary, pre-registered** | every usable pitch | log loss over take-ball / take-strike / whiff / foul / in play, given count, base-out at the PA start, level, side, late-and-close |
| `pitch_swing_take`, `pitch_cstrike_given_take`, `pitch_result_given_swing` | diagnostic | same pitches | the exact chain decomposition of `pitch_5way` (they add up to it, event by event) |
| `pa_5way` | secondary | plate appearances | K / BB / HBP / hit / out-in-play from 0-0. It is a function of the pitch events, so not independent evidence |
| `bip_type_outs` | secondary | outs with a written type | ground / line / fly / pop given an out (and the count) |
| `bip_result` | secondary | non-bunt balls in play | out / 1B / 2B / 3B / HR / error given in play (and the count) |
| `bip_type_known` | diagnostic (biased) | balls in play with a written type | the 4 types |
| `runs_pa_sq`, `runs_pitch_state_sq` | **reported only** | PAs / pitch states | squared error of runs to the end of the inning vs RE (+ CV). Not a hitter metric: no hitter-model term enters it |

Every mean is reported with three bootstrap SEs (section 7):
- by game;
- by home team;
- by both teams (pigeonhole).

Every version is also reported as a paired difference against each reference (section 9).

## 2. Data and filters

Source: `C:/dev/pitchiq-data/college/college.sqlite3`, opened read-only. Canonical games only.

### 2.1 Which level-seasons are scored
- **Full game:** at least 90% of PAs lettered, counting a lettered game's "(0-0)" first-pitch PAs as complete
  (`quality.py`'s corrected definition).
- **Harness rule:** at least 100 full games and at least 5,000 PAs whose pitch events pass **every** filter. Since the
  review the PA check is made after the build, not from `game_qc`. On today's archive this scores D1 2006-2025, D2
  2015-2025 and D3 2024-2025.
- **Plan rule (plan section 6):** a level is held to "beat v1.2" only if at least 50% of its games are full in **both**
  2025 and 2026. On the corrected definition, 2025 / 2026 shares are D1 0.69 / 0.86 (passes), D2 0.34 / 0.73 (fails),
  D3 0.16 / 0.74 (fails). These numbers come from `coverage.csv`, which counts 2026 games without loading them.
  **By the plan, only D1 can be held to the pass rule today.** D2 and D3 are scored for information, and their pass
  rule waits until their 2025 coverage reaches 50% or the plan changes.
- **One game set:** only full games are scored, for every metric, so there is one bootstrap. `--selection-check`
  compares K / BB / HBP / hit / HR in full vs other games.
- **2026 is never loaded.** Every season-loading path calls `assert_not_heldout`: `games_for`, `build_season`,
  `prequalify`, `selection_check`, `fit_seasons_for`, the season parser (ranges, `--fit-seasons`) and
  `harness_truth.real_cells`. Tests check each one.

### 2.2 Which PAs and pitches
| Rule | Pitch events | PA outcome | BIP | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IBB, catcher's / batter's interference | out | out | n/a | in |
| PA ends in a bunt (`bip_type = bunt`, SAC, SH) | out | out | out | in |
| Letters with `count_ok != 1` | out | in | in | in |
| No letters, count not 0-0 | out | in | in | in |
| "(0-0)" in play or HBP, no letters (full game) | in, 1-pitch PA | in | in | in |
| Letter outside B K S F H (N, P) | out | in | in | in |
| Our replay disagrees with the result | out | in | in | in |
| A half-inning whose last PA doesn't reach 3 outs (walk-off, shortened, ended on a caught stealing / pickoff, or a parse gap) | in | in | in | out |
| PA with a mid-PA runner event | in (state at the PA start) | in | in | out for pitch-state runs |
| An out with no written type (mostly FC) | in | in | out of `bip_type_outs` only (counted: `out_type_unknown:*`) | in |

**Pitch replay** (`pitch_events`):
- B = ball, K = called strike, S = whiff, F = foul (no strike added at two strikes), H = HBP.
- An F that is the last letter of a strikeout, at two strikes, is a foul tip and is scored as a whiff.
- The replay must end in a way that agrees with the result:
  - in play: at exactly the printed count, then one in-play pitch is added;
  - strikeout: at strike three;
  - walk: at ball four;
  - HBP: an H letter, or the printed count with the HBP added.

**Late and close** = inning ≥ scheduled innings − 2 and margin ≤ 2, the engine's own big-spot rule.
- Scheduled innings are inferred per game: a game whose last inning is 7, or 8 without a 10-run final margin, counts as
  a 7-inning game. Anything else is 9.
- v1.2 sees the flag as inning 9 of 9, margin 0.

## 3. The pitcher assumption (identical for every engine version)

Box scores have no pitch type or location. Every version is asked about the same pitches, by count.

**Primary: `calib_world`** (`HARNESS_ASSUMPTION_VERSION = "h2-calibworld-2026-11-21"`, the review decision). This is
v1.2's own `calib.py` world, frozen once by `harness_freeze.py` (source commit f2042fa) into `harness_frozen.py`:
- the simulator's default staff:
  - starter FB / SL / CH, 0.65 of pitches;
  - reliever FB / CB / CH, 0.35 of pitches;
  - grades 2-4, command 2-4, confidence 0.55-0.80;
- the engine's own calls (`call_v08`, table solved at 0-0 per pitcher and side, 0 outs, bases empty);
- where those calls land in that world, including the hidden hitters' off-day wildness;
- the result weighted by how often each count is reached (calib's visits).

It is stored per level × side × count as (pitcher, pitch, target, landed zone, weight), with the pitch specs and each
count's same-side share. D2 is the mean of D1 and D3.

v1.2's level parameters were fit (`calib.py` → `outcome_params.py`) so that exactly this world hits real K / BB / HBP.
So v1.2 is scored in the world it was calibrated in. On a 60-game 2024 sample:

| Level | | Ball + HBP | Called strike | Whiff | Foul | In play | K per PA | BB per PA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | real | 38.7% | 18.5% | 10.4% | 15.8% | 16.7% | 21.3% | 11.4% |
| D1 | v1.2, calib world | 41.7% | 13.7% | 9.5% | 16.4% | 18.7% | 18.1% | 11.0% |
| D1 | v1.2, old hand-set pitcher | 48.8% | 11.6% | 5.4% | 16.4% | 17.8% | 10.5% | 19.3% |

**How the assumption is used:**
- The adapter is asked how the engine's **average unknown hitter** (no grades, tags, reads or history) responds to each
  frozen pitch landed in each zone. The harness weights the answers.
- An engine version's own call policy and landing model never enter the score.
- Checked: one-zone integration reproduces `outcome_probs` exactly. It is linear in the landing shares to 1e-16, and the
  test is to 1e-5.

**Variants** (`--variants`, sensitivity only):
- `handset`: the retired h1 assumption — a 3/3 pitcher with hand-set fastball shares and targets on the frozen
  `harness_landing.py` shares;
- `calib_more_fb` / `calib_less_fb`: the frozen mix with the fastball share ±0.10.

Each is scored raw and recalibrated.

**Pitches are independent within a PA:** no sequencing, and the state is the one at the PA start.

## 4. Metrics in detail

**Pitch events.**
- Five ways: HBP folds into take-ball, and the wild-pitch ball counts as a ball.
- Reported overall and by count. The by-count difference against the first reference has a game-bootstrap SE.
- The decomposition is diagnostic: swing / take, called strike given a take, result given a swing.

**Ball in play.**
- Outs nearly always carry a type; hits often don't: 27% of balls in play are "unknown", almost all hits.
- The type metric is therefore the type of outs: P(type | out, count). The engine side is P(type) × P(out | type),
  normalised.
- `bip_result` checks the hit / out side: P(result | in play, count).
- The BIP labels are the scorer's words, not Statcast's (risk R9).

**PA outcome.** The version's per-count pitch probabilities are chained from 0-0, and P(hit | in play, count) splits
the in-play share.

**Runs.**
- `runs_rest_inning` from the PA start is scored against RE(outs, bases), and from a pitch state against RE + CV.
- Halves whose last PA doesn't reach 3 outs are excluded.
- Reported only.

## 5. The adapter interface

`Tables` holds, per data level:
- `P6[count, outs, bases, side, late, 6]`;
- `BIP[..., 4 types, 6 results]`;
- `RE[3, 8]`, `CV[12]`.

Every metric is derived from these; `check_tables` rejects bad tables.

An engine version subclasses `EngineAdapter` and implements:
- `zone_responses(level, b, k, outs, bases, same, late, needs)`, for each (pitcher, pitch, target, zone) in `needs`;
- `run_tables(level)`.

Every adapter carries `fit_seasons`, the college seasons it was fit on, and `score_season` refuses to score any of them
(review finding 6).

**v1.2** (`EngineV12Adapter`):
- `engine.outcome_probs` with `engine.landing` swapped for a one-zone landing (restored after);
- `type_mix` × `OUT_TABLE`;
- `RE_BY_LEVEL` / `CV_BY_LEVEL`.

D2 is scored at D1 settings (`v1.2`) and at D3 settings (`v1.2@D3`).

**Build fingerprint.** Each output records the engine's git sha (`+dirty` if an engine file is modified) and a
sha256 of `OUTCOME_ADJ`. **The v1.3 build to score is the one after its pitcher-side recalibration (`fit_all.py`),**
because `OUTCOME_ADJ` enters `outcome_probs`.

## 6. References and baselines

All of these are fit on up to 8 seasons strictly before the scored season, recency-weighted (each season's weight
halves every 2 seasons back, as planned for v1.3).
- Never the scored season, never a later one, never 2026: D3 2024's fit uses D3 2016-2023 only.
- `v1.2_recal` (**the pass-rule reference**, review finding 1): v1.2's tables with the same mean-preserving per-count
  recalibration a v1.3 count layer applies.
  - Per count, the 6 pitch outcomes (72 numbers) and P(type, result | in play) (288 numbers) are raked so that, over the
    fit contexts, each count's mean equals the fit seasons' frequency.
  - Runs are v1.2's.
  - D2 also gets `v1.2@D3_recal`.
- `base_flat`: one mix for everything (the floor).
- `base_count`: per-count frequencies; P(type | result, count) shrunk to the pooled value.
- `base_count_bo`: per count × outs × bases, shrunk to `base_count`.

**What the baseline shows (section 12): `v1.2_recal` equals `base_count` to about 4 decimals in every level-season.**
Within a count, v1.2's context terms (base-out, side, late) barely move its predictions. So under this harness, the
pass rule asks whether v1.3 predicts better than **per-count frequencies**. Only context and its zone / pitch parts'
mix-weighted average can buy a gain. That is the honest bar; it is not a weakness of the reference.

## 7. Bootstrap

- **Three schemes**, each B = 1,000 with a fixed seed:
  - games;
  - home teams (a game's weight = its home team's draw count);
  - both teams (one draw per team; a game's weight = draw(home) × draw(away), the pigeonhole bootstrap).
- **Teams** are keyed on a normalised name word set. Two spellings of one school can still split, which only makes the
  team SE slightly smaller.
- **The SE used for any rule is the largest of the three.** On this data it is always the two-team SE: a median 2.6×
  the game SE on the primary metric's paired difference (range 1.9-4.6× over the 33 level-seasons). Home-team
  clustering alone gives about 1.5×, matching the reviewer's measurement.
- **Paired differences** use the same resamples.
- The by-count differences carry a game SE. They are descriptive, not a rule.

## 9. The pass rule (as proposed for Step 6)

- **Primary metric:** `pitch_5way`, per level. Pre-registered here before any v1.3 score exists.
- **Reference:** `v1.2_recal`, fit on seasons before the scored one. Raw v1.2 is reported for information.
- **Pass:** v1.3 − `v1.2_recal` < −2 × SE, where SE is the largest of the three clustered SEs, **and** the improvement is
  at least **0.002 nats per pitch** (`MIN_EFFECT`; **proposed, Drew decides**). For scale, on D1 v1.2 − `v1.2_recal`
  is +0.018 to +0.029 per season, and the clustered SE of that paired difference in 2025 is 0.0008 (D2 0.0017,
  D3 0.0034). So with the full D1 data, 2 SE alone would accept gains of about 0.0015 nats per pitch.
- **D2:** v1.3 must beat **both** `v1.2_recal` (D1 settings) and `v1.2@D3_recal`.
  - Pre-registered: the better of the two raw settings is the one with the lower 2025 `pitch_5way`. Section 12 names it.
  - The recalibrated pair is nearly identical (section 6).
- **Which levels:** only levels passing the plan rule (section 2.1) are held to it; today that is D1.
- **When:** only on 2026, once, at Step 6. 2025 is used to choose and gate.

## 10. Known-truth check (Single-A 2025)

`python -m hitter_data.harness_truth`. Single-A Statcast 2025, from the zone-seed cache: 168,260 pitches. The engine's
own bands and the FB / off-speed class are known. The steps:
1. Take v1.2's zone responses (D1 settings) under the frozen D1 calib-world mix.
2. Rake per count to the real Single-A per-count frequencies, as `v1.2_recal` does.
3. Score both on the real pitches.

| Log loss per pitch | v1.2 | v1.2_recal | truth (in-sample cell frequencies) |
|---|---|---|---|
| given count only (what the college harness sees) | 1.4875 | 1.4716 | 1.4716 |
| given count + class + band (what the live engine needs) | 1.1803 | 1.1596 | 1.1109 |

Rates by band, pooled over counts and classes, weighted by real pitches:

| Band | Swing rate (real / v1.2 / recal) | Whiff per swing (real / v1.2 / recal) | Called strike per take (real / v1.2 / recal) |
|---|---|---|---|
| heart | .756 / .697 / **.659** | .126 / .113 / **.142** | .998 / .980 / .985 |
| edge | .555 / .503 / .518 | .231 / .219 / .261 | .630 / .291 / .368 |
| off | .284 / .303 / **.345** | .499 / .431 / .477 | .022 / .028 / .040 |
| noncomp | .090 / .058 / .070 | .856 / .601 / .638 | .001 / .000 / .000 |

**Landing.** The frozen D1 calib-world landing is heart-heavy and edge-light against Single-A. For example:
- 0-0: frozen .31 / .27 / .27 / .15 vs real .21 / .38 / .25 / .15;
- 3-0: frozen .44 / .23 / .20 / .12 vs real .24 / .39 / .24 / .13.

**Reading:**
- The per-count recalibration improves the total, including the zone-conditional loss.
- It does so partly by learning the wrong zone behaviour. To match per-count swing rates with too many pitches over the
  heart, it **lowers** heart swing (.697 → .659; real .756) and **raises** chase swing (.303 → .345; real .284).
- That is the E7 trap, measured.
- Single-A pitchers aren't college pitchers, so the landing gap is indicative, not exact. But the mechanism is general.

**Consequences:**
- (1) A v1.3 count layer must be fit relative to a landing it can defend: the zone table's Single-A seed should set zone
  shapes, and the count layer only the count level.
- (2) **Required pre-v1.3 check:** the band table above, for v1.3, must not move away from real on Single-A relative to
  v1.2.
- (3) An option for Drew: re-freeze the harness landing from real Single-A bands per count (class × band) instead of the
  calib world. Identical for every version, closer to real pitchers; v1.2 would no longer be scored in its own world.

## 12. v1.2 baseline (every qualifying season ≤ 2025)

Command in section 13. 33 level-seasons scored (D1 2006-2025, D2 2015-2025, D3 2024-2025), 166 s. Raw JSON and log:
`C:/dev/pitchiq-data/harness/v12_baseline_2026-11-21.json` and `.log`. 2026 was never loaded.

**Headlines:**
- **Raw v1.2 in the calib world is worse than per-count frequencies, by about 0.02 nats per pitch.**
  - D1 2025: v1.2 1.4678 vs `v1.2_recal` 1.4470, difference +0.0208 (SE 0.0008).
  - D2 2025: +0.0188 (SE 0.0017).
  - D3 2025: +0.0260 (SE 0.0034).
- **But v1.2 beats `base_flat` everywhere** (by about 0.03), so its count pattern is directionally right. The
  reviewer's "v1.2 worse than `base_flat`" came from the old hand-set assumption: under it, raw v1.2 is 1.4999 on D1
  2025.
- **Where the gap is.** The D1 2025 by-count difference against `v1.2_recal` is largest in the counts where the batter
  is ahead: 2-0 +0.067, 1-0 +0.055, 3-0 +0.054, 0-0 +0.028. With two strikes it is +0.003 to +0.009. This matches
  section 10: the calib-world landing puts too many pitches over the heart when the batter is ahead.
- **Decomposition, D1 2025** (raw v1.2 / recal): swing/take 0.659 / 0.652, called strike given take 0.607 / 0.589,
  result given swing 1.081 / 1.073. The called-strike part is the largest single gap.
- **`v1.2_recal` = `base_count`** to within 0.0001 in all 33 level-seasons (section 6). It also equals `base_count` under
