# PitchIQ V1.3: pitch strategy and game theory audit

Auditor: pitch strategy / game theory specialist. Tree: `/home/claude/pitch-caller` @ c602f1c (read-only). v1.2.3 reference:
a separate `--depth 1` clone of `main` (8bcf909) at `/home/claude/audit/gt/main`. Scripts: `/home/claude/audit/gt/scripts/`,
sim logs: `/home/claude/audit/gt/runs_*`. All runs were small and used fixed seeds. "D1u" means runs ÷ run_scale (HS run_scale 1.669).

## 1. Verdict
**Grade C-.** The mechanics are sound. The quantal-response solve converges (residual ≤ 1e-3 everywhere tested), Python
and JS match, the 3-0 / 3-1 no-chase rule holds, and the setup tie-break never gives up value. But the "game theory" does not
make the calls. Outside two strikes the hitter's guess is uniform to within 1 point. The hitter-guess coefficients were never
fitted. At HS the fastball share comes from the percentile floor table on 63% of pitches. In the engine's own model, the
minimax mix beats the engine's mix. A hard rule (never re-call the pitch just shaken off) leaks in the all-shaken fallback.

## 3. Scenarios and tests run (all on c602f1c unless marked main)
- **Equilibrium per state** (`eq_check.py LEVEL ARSENAL [bases outs]`). Configurations: D3 three; D1 three and four; HS three
  and FB/FC/CB; D3 two; D3 three with R1+R3, 1 out; D2 four with R2+R3, 2 out (84 count states).
  Exploitability X (best pure sit vs the engine's σ): **0.0005–0.0147 runs/pitch**. Largest at 0-1 / 1-1 with runners on and at
  HS 0-2 / 1-2 / 3-2. Count-weighted, that is **0.35–0.46 runs/game at college and 0.30–0.52 HS runs/game** (`per_game.py`, 145 / 125 pitches per game).
  A hitter who knows each pitch gains 1.1–2.6 runs/game in the model. Residuals ≤ 0.0010 in every table and root solve.
- **v1.2.3 (main) for comparison**, same scripts with `ENG=main`. X 0.56 D3 / 0.56 D1 / 0.35 HS runs/game, close to v1.3. Mixing cost vs
  best call: 1.43 D3 / 1.01 D1 / 1.41 HS runs/game, versus 0.47 / 0.48 / 0.91 in v1.3 (ruling 10 halved the college temperature).
  The sit game was equally inert in v1.2.3 (H/Hmax ≥ 0.98 outside two strikes). HS mix-vs-pure-under-best-response was negative
  there too (−0.40). Top pitch type at two strikes: FB in v1.2.3, slider in v1.3.
- **Minimax** (`minimax.py`, scipy HiGHS LP over the engine's own M): see GT-3.
- **Rule conflicts** (`conflicts.py`, `hot_floor.py`): floor vs landing-hot, shake-off, mashed, FB wild, E-7 state. Results:
  - Shake-off of the fastball at HS 3-2 (FB/FC/CB): the cutter absorbs the whole floor (cutter 0.55, 0.082 runs behind best). Per ruling 16, but a coach shaking the fastball probably wanted the curve.
  - FB and cutter middle / down-middle mashed twice: the floor moves 55% onto the non-mashed fastball spots, up to 0.122 runs behind best.
  - FB wild (2/7) at HS 2-2: the not-landing block is off (not behind in the count), so the floor still forces 40% fastball (+0.006). At 2-1 and 3-2 the block wins: 0% FB, and 3-2 becomes deterministic slider 1.00.
  - E-7 state after the fix (HS 1-2, opposite side, grade-4 slider): slider 0.57, FB 0.33 = floor, +0.011 runs vs raw.
  - No floor-vs-no-chase conflict: the floor only sees allowed targets.
- **Predictability** (`predict.py`). D3: 3 games, 585 pitches (`LEVEL=college_d3 batch.py runs_d3 1 3`). HS: 3 games, 503 pitches.
  Oracle top-1 (a coach who could reproduce the engine's mix) 0.645 / 0.631. Count-only leave-one-out predictor 0.53 / 0.55.
  Count + previous pitch 0.55 / 0.55. Always fastball-class 0.51 / 0.57. Mean pitch-type entropy 1.05 / 1.02 bits.
  Near-deterministic counts: college 3-0 (top type ≥ 80% on 100% of D3 3-0 pitches) and HS 3-0 (0.89). No other count is above 0.40.
  **v1.2.3 (main) D3**, 3 games, 577 pitches: oracle 0.659, count-only 0.53, count + previous 0.50, always-FB 0.53. P(same as previous)
  **0.47** vs 0.35 if independent. 4th-straight pitches **3.6%**. So v1.3 is a little streakier (0.52 / 6.2%) and about equally predictable by count.
  Sims can't measure the *cost* of predictability: the simulated hitter sits by the fixed v0.7c count prior
  (`engine.py:2106-2107`, `sit_sigma` never set by `simulate.py`) and never adapts to the engine.
- **Stakes-scaled randomness**: T is tightest at 3-0 / 3-2 (0.005·k) and 2-2 (0.006–0.010·k), loosest at 0-1 / 1-1 (0.015·k).
  This matches the owner's ordering. Pitch-type entropy stays 0.91–0.99 of maximum in those counts with equal-grade arsenals.
- **JS spot check** (`js/spot.js`, `app/engine.js` = `web/lib/engine.js` byte-identical): HS FB/FC/CB 3-2 gives CB 0.455 / FB 0.356 / FC 0.190, and the GT-1 shake
  state gives SL 0.583. Both match Python exactly. Minor: `app/engine.js:1073` holds a dead, unfloored `policyFromCosts`, shadowed by the one at line 1188.
